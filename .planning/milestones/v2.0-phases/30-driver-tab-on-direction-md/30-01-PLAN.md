<objective>
Make launcher runs the Driver tab's run list. The selected run's status comes from the tool-written frontmatter
plus the run-lock probe (`DerivedStatus`), so a run whose frontmatter says `running` while its lock is free shows
as crashed. Watcher events under `.planning/meta-manager/drivers/` refresh the list without a full project
re-parse. Legacy Phase 17/18 runs collapse into one read-only row, the 28-07 bottom section goes, and `x` kills
the selected run through the existing confirmation (D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-09, D-11, D-13, D-22, D-24, D-25).

Purpose: the tracer for DTAB-01/DTAB-02. Plans 30-02..30-05 fill the selected-run view, the dashboard,
steering and the lifecycle into this composition.

Output: `src/ui/screens/driver_run.rs`, the `ChangeKind::DriverRun`/`Ignored` route, `Action::LauncherRunsRefreshed`,
`AppContext::schedule_launcher_refresh`, the new Driver footer and help rows, and their tests.
</objective>
