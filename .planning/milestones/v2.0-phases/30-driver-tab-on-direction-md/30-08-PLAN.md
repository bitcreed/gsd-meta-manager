The full coverage table for plans 30-06..30-09 is in 30-06-PLAN.md. RESEARCH Item 1 had kept the shipped no-split
spawn ("a split would need a new dependency or a naive split"); this plan takes the third option the review names,
`sh -c '"$EDITOR" "$@"'`, which needs no dependency and keeps quoted paths with spaces working, so it supersedes that
recommendation `[inferred — audit]`.

Why not "write to a temp file, then rename" for ETXTBSY: the shim is already persisted through a temp file and a
rename (`src/launcher/config_dir.rs` `write_atomic`) and still raced. A rename keeps the inode, and the inherited write
descriptor (a sibling test thread's fork, open until that child execs) points at that same inode. Only keeping other
threads from forking during the window, or retrying the exec, removes the race.
