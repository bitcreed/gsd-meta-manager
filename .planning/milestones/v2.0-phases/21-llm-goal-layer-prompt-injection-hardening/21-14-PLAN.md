<objective>
Make the guard layer honest and remove the two structural smells 21-PREMISES.md adjudicated
against it. Three of round 4's confirmed findings are defects **in the guards themselves**
(WR-01 guard six's unstated live blind region, WR-02 guard eight naming no limits, WR-03's
tautology — the last closed by 21-13); the round-4 verifier's key nuance is that textual
guards are approximations of properties and must either become structural or become honest.

This plan does both, per Premise 3 (QUESTIONABLE) and Premise 2 (collision debt due):

1. **Guard six**: the marker-to-EOF skip stops being silent — a new tree-wide assertion fails
   on any column-zero item after a `mod tests {` marker, and the one live instance
   (`count_backlog_items`) moves above the marker (also clearing the clippy lint that
   corroborated the finding). Limits 4 and 5 join the header in the established register.
2. **Guard eight**: gains the limits block its sibling was rewritten to model, plus real
   closures for its two cheapest gaps (`Self::` needles, a variant-import assertion).
3. **The name collision**: `src/driver/run.rs`'s second `enum CommandSource` becomes
   `IterationSource`; guard eight then asserts single-declaration instead of dodging by
   needle shape. The same match's `(None, None) => CommandSource::Fixed(String::new())` arm —
   which manufactures the exact blank value this phase exists to refuse, as a fallback for a
   state believed unreachable — becomes a typed `Err(DriveError::NoCommandSource)`.
4. **No silent drops**: the three round-2 carryovers adjudicated OUT (Premise 6) are recorded
   in `deferred-items.md` with reasons.

Output: every textual guard in the suite names its approximations with directions, its silent
directions are bounded or made loud, one enum name means one thing, and no production match
arm can fabricate a blank command.
</objective>
