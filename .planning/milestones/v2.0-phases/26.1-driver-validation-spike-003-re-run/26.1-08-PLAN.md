## Why r261-run2 converged (evidence)

**Cause.** The feed advances on the executor's own SUMMARY commit, which is not the executor's last
action. In r261-run2 the executor stayed alive 62 s after that commit. In that window it saw the
feed test go red, implemented the new `+` rule and re-committed. The verifier then spawned against
a feed the code already passed. r261a met its gap only because its executor exited 8 s after its
SUMMARY commit.

- Trigger definition. `tools/drift.py:226-231` (`count_summaries`: generation = number of `*-SUMMARY.md` files in HEAD) and `tools/drift.py:265-272` (`poll` rewrites the feed when that count changes).
- Advance during the executor. `experiment/results/r261-run2/drift.log:2` has `02:32:27Z gen=1 summaries=1 head=0b9a262 titles=27`. `0b9a262` is the executor's SUMMARY commit `docs(01-01): complete slugify plan` (`results/r261-run2/scratch-git-log.txt:12`).
- Absorption before verification. `results/r261-run2/scratch-git-log.txt:8` is `c7b489b fix(01-01): spell out '+' as 'plus' to match regenerated feed house style`, and `:5` is `bb62bab docs(01-01): update summary for feed re-sync fix`. Both carry the executor's `01-01` scope.
- The verifier saw an absorbed feed. `results/r261-run2/drift.log:3` has `02:34:11Z snapshot ... status=passed gen=1`. The worker report confirms the order: `results/r261-run2/LOG.md:58` ("A gsd-executor ran plan 01-01 (3 tasks), then a gsd-verifier ran") and `:64` ("The feed grew from 21 to 27 lines mid-run, so `+` now maps to \"plus\" (c7b489b)"). There was one executor and one verifier (`results/r261-run2/dup-report.txt:3`).
- Timing from the uncommitted scratch transcripts (metadata only: tool_use timestamps, file times and commit times, in `~/.local/state/gsd-meta-manager/spike-003/r261-run2-20261018T022502Z/`):
  - gsd-executor spawned at 02:31:05.029Z, and its transcript was last written at 02:33:29Z;
  - the commits 0b9a262 (02:32:27Z), c7b489b (02:32:52Z) and bb62bab (02:33:20Z) all fall inside that lifetime;
  - gsd-verifier spawned at 02:33:31.810Z, an `Agent` tool_use with `subagent_type` `gsd-verifier` in worker `agent-ab09ea1c8aefeb511.jsonl`, and its own transcript was born at 02:33:31.91Z.
- Contrast with r261a, which met a gap:
  - `results/r261a/drift.log:2` has gen=1 at 02:06:22Z (SUMMARY commit 81dc1f8);
  - its executor's transcript ended at 02:06:30Z, 8 s later, with no fix commit;
  - its verifier spawned at 02:06:33.628Z;
  - `results/r261a/drift.log:3` has `02:07:06Z ... status=gaps_found`.
- Rejected trigger. `subagents/agent-*.meta.json` (dup_check's agent-type source) is written when a subagent ends: the verifier's meta was born at 02:34:18Z, after its snapshot. So meta.json cannot be the trigger. The parent transcript's `Agent` tool_use line is the earliest host-visible signal.

**Design against the cause (seed v2).** The feed advances once per gsd-verifier spawn. That is
always after every executor of the round has returned (execute-phase spawns the verifier in the
foreground after the executors), and before the verifier's first read. Nothing on the executor side
ever sees the new rule before a verification. Each round's verifier sees exactly one new
house-rule class, so rounds 1, 2 and 3 end `gaps_found` with strictly increasing gens. The live
proof is the Haiku gate in 26.1-10 (first verification `gaps_found`).
