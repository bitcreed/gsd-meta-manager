<objective>
Ship the Driver tab's frame: an 11th tab that is actually visible, a two-pane run list and run
detail, the header that makes OBS-03 real, and the reused pipeline row.

Purpose: this is where TRANS-05's "watches its output, current step, and elapsed time" gets a place
to live, and where OBS-03's goal becomes readable. Two traps are already known and both are
mechanical rather than aesthetic. First, **the ten shipped tabs already occupy 96 cells**, so at an
80-column terminal the last two are silently dropped off the right edge — the unresolved "tab bar
overflow at 80 columns" blocker carried in STATE.md since Phase 12. Appending an 11th takes the bar
to 107 cells and the Driver tab is the *last* entry, so at every common width it would be the tab
that never renders. Second, a percentage-only constraint on the detail pane is exactly what clipped
the trailing `[V]` of the D-R-P-E-V cell (UIFIX-02 / CR-01), and this plan renders that same widget
in a narrower pane.

**Module-layout decision, recorded (Claude's Discretion in 18-CONTEXT):** the Driver tab's render and
key logic goes in its own `src/ui/screens/driver.rs`, with `detail.rs` delegating. Every other
sub-tab renders inline, but every other sub-tab is about forty lines and this one is eight
width/height tiers, a ring-buffer renderer and a four-state injection widget; `detail.rs` is already
5,206 lines. The alternative — inline, matching every sibling — was declined for that reason, and the
decision is written into the new module's header doc.

Output: the six tab sites, `tab_titles` with its width tiers and windowing, the two-pane layout, the
run list, the header, the reused pipeline row, and the one-row step timeline.
</objective>
