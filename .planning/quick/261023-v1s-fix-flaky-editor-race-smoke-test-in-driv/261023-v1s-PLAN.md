<objective>
Find and fix the root cause of the flaky `tests/driverctl_race.rs::editor_race_smoke`. It failed in about 11 of 200 container stress runs, and 17/600 and 11/600 in the `other` column of quick 261023-qs8's binary-mode runs. The panic message is `inplace: the tool must have spliced at least once`, for example with `tool={"gave-up": 13, "skipped-insane-body": 4}`. The order is strict: harness and baseline on the unmodified tree, root cause proven by measurement and intervention, product-vs-test verdict, fix, then identical AFTER stress, scan of the sibling race/timing tests, and the release gates.

Purpose: gate 4 of the release dry run must be deterministic. A flaky gate either blocks a tag or trains the operator to rerun until green, and rerunning until green is how a real splice/editor defect (D-23 exchange-verify, D-13/D-20 lock) would slip through. This plan therefore decides, with evidence, whether the in-place starvation is designed behaviour or a product bug.

Planner hypothesis (to be tested, NOT assumed): in the in-place case each splice attempt's window, from the read of DIRECTION.md to the exchange, contains `write_temp`'s `sync_all`. In the container that costs about 3-27 ms on overlay under fsync contention. The in-place human saves about every 1 ms (a uniform 0-2 ms sleep plus an unsynced `fs::write`). So nearly every attempt sees the body change, the verify mismatches, and after SPLICE_TRIES=5 attempts plus 50 ms of backoff the splice returns GaveUp. With about 17 regens per second, sometimes none lands in the 1 s case. If that holds, GaveUp is the designed human-wins outcome and the test's `ok > 0` witness carries a timing assumption.

Output: harness and tallies, 261023-v1s-EVIDENCE.md, the fix (test-side in tests/driverctl_race.rs, or product-side in src/driver/direction.rs or src/driver/lock.rs with a red-first regression test), any scan-driven fixes (each a recorded deviation naming its file), and the SUMMARY with before/after rates. The two src/ files in files_modified change permanently ONLY on the product branch. Temporary probes and mutants touch them and are always reverted.
