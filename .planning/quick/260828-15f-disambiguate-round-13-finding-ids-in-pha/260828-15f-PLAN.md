`detail.rs:1692-1707`; round-13 CR-01 is the `--resume`/`-r` successor-arity defect. A bare
"WR-04" is therefore ambiguous phase-wide and a future round could act on the wrong finding.

name "WR-05-new" to keep round 13's `--` finding apart from the historical WR-05.

defer the collision, because the root cause is that each review round restarts its numbering

self-describing, and it establishes a corollary the document then states outright: inside
21-REVIEW.md a bare id means the phase's pre-existing/historical finding of that name, an
`R13-` id means this round's.
