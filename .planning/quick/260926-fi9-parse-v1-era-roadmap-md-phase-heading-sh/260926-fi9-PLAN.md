## Inferred decisions (for audit)

1. **[INFERRED] Two channels, split by region, not by shape.** Phase lines inside a *closed-milestone*
   `<details>` block go to `parse_shipped_phases` / `ProjectState::shipped_phases` (display-only). All
   other lines go to `parse_roadmap_phases` / `phases` (GSD-facing). Reasons:
   - GSD parity: `stripClosedMilestoneDetails` drops these blocks before GSD enumerates phases.
   - Pinned tests: `ttbook_fixture_gsd_phase_list_is_unchanged_by_build_phase_support`
     (roadmap_md.rs:1950, `["8".."13"]`), `parse_project_state_reads_planned_build_phases`
     (mod.rs:1522) and `ttbook_phase13_phase_progress_is_5_of_6` (mod.rs:1337,
     `roadmap_entries().len() == 11`) all exclude ttbook's shipped v1 lines.
   - Hazards: aiFlowAgent phases 1-3 and wordoclock v1.0/v1.1 phases have no directory anywhere.
     In `phases` they would infer `NoDirectory` and take the current-phase cell (mod.rs:891-935).
     They would also become legal driver goal targets (`src/driver/run.rs:2188`).
2. **[INFERRED] Closed = GSD's rule, ported verbatim.** A `<details>` block is closed when its first
   `<summary>…</summary>` text matches `(?i)\b(?:CLOSED|ARCHIVED|ABANDONED|SHIPPED|FAILED)\b|✅|🗄` and not
   `(?i)\b(?:STARTED|ACTIVE|WIP)\b|in\s+progress|🚧|🔄`. A block with no summary, and an unterminated
   block, are not closed. `test_parse_roadmap_details_wrapped` (`<summary>Completed phases</summary>`)
   stays GSD-facing and unchanged.
3. **[INFERRED] Name merge stays first-non-empty.** "Prefer the richer entry" is honoured field by field
   exactly as `merge_duplicate_phases` already does: the detail heading's plans (max) and depends (only
   it has them) win, the checkbox `[x]` fills completion (OR), and the checklist description fills the
   heading's empty one. The name is NOT switched to the heading's. That would rename this repo's phases 15/17
   and break "existing bold behavior unchanged".
4. **[INFERRED] Leading `(TAG)` groups after a bold title fold into the name.** For example
   `**Phase 03.1: X** (INSERTED) - d` gives name `X (INSERTED)` and desc `d`, so mailbot's checklist and
   heading spell the same name and its displayed title does not change.
5. **[INFERRED] Plan tallies are parsed, for new shapes only.** `(6/6 plans)` gives completed 6 / total 6;
   `(3 plans, complete)` gives 3/3; `(1 plan)` gives 0/1. Existing-grammar lines (checklist_re) never
   parse a tally, so their counts stay byte-identical.
6. **[INFERRED] Completion without a checkbox.** A bare line is complete iff its tally carries
   `complete`/`completed`/`done`. A heading inside a closed collapse is complete iff it has at least
   one plan item and all of them are checked. Otherwise not complete: no guessing from "the milestone shipped".
7. **[INFERRED] Bare `Phase N: Name` lines are recognized only inside a closed collapse** (this repo's
   own v1.0-v1.2 shape). Outside one, a bare line is prose.
8. **[INFERRED] Dash headings are accepted although none were observed**, because the task asks for
   them. The separator must be spaced (`\s+(?:--|-|–|—)\s+`), so `### Phase 3 Implementation Scope`
   stays prose. The shared `phase_heading_re()` gains the form, so goals and sections work. `roadmap_milestones`'
   phase-heading regex gains it too, so `### Phase 2 - Migrate to v2 API` is not read as a milestone.
9. **[INFERRED] Across channels the GSD entry wins and nothing is merged**, mirroring
   `roadmap_entries()`' planned rule. This protects GSD's `--reset-phase-numbers` (new-milestone.md:26).
10. **[INFERRED] Shipped rows: marker from the checkbox only, never `Current`.** They get no disk
    inference (archived dirs are often deleted). A shipped phase whose id no milestone range covers
    is omitted from the model rather than guessed into the active, synthetic or band-less area. The
    survey found none uncovered.
11. **[INFERRED] The synthetic fixture lives in a new `tests/fixtures/roadmap-shapes/`**, so the
    vendored-fixture guard `vendored_roadmap_fixtures_are_sanitised` (list and goal count) stays byte-identical.
12. **[INFERRED] Out of scope:** GSD template plan items without `PLAN.md` (`- [ ] 01-01: desc`),
    `**Phase 2:** Name` (colon inside bold, unobserved), and adding shipped ids to
    `parse_planned_build_phases`' known_ids.
