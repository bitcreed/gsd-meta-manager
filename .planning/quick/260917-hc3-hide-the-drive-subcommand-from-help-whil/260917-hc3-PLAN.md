<objective>
Hide the `drive` subcommand from `--help` while keeping it fully executable.

Quick task 260917-fko put 28 driver surfaces behind `GSDMM_EXPERIMENTAL_FEATURES` but
deliberately left the `drive` CLI subcommand ungated, because the TUI respawns itself as
`current_exe() drive …` and env propagation through the envelope's env scrub is not
guaranteed. That leaves `drive` advertised in `--help` as a thing a user is meant to type,
which is exactly what the flag was supposed to stop.

**D-01 — the fix is `#[command(hide = true)]` on the `Commands::Drive` variant.** Clap runs
hidden subcommands normally; `hide` removes an entry from the rendered help and from nothing
else. This is the treatment `Commands::Envelope` already carries three lines further down in
the same file, and `src/cli.rs:276-287` already argues at length why `hide = true` alone —
never `#[cfg(debug_assertions)]` — is right for a subcommand another process re-enters.

**D-02 — `drive` must keep running.** It is not made to refuse, the spawn path is not
touched, the envelope env scrub is not touched, and it is not gated on the experimental flag.

Purpose: keep `--help` honest about what a user is meant to type, without touching the one
path that has to keep working.
Output: one clap attribute, two tests, one corrected documentation bullet.
</objective>
