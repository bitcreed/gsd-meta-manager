Its `Phase 3-01:` lines match no recognizer, `PhaseNum::parse("3-01")` abstains, and a phase `3` borrows `03-02-beta`'s plans. As a result, the dashboard, Roadmap tab and driver are blind or wrong on it, while GSD's own router (oracle 1.20.1, measured) reads it correctly.

Output: convention-aware recognizers, M-NN PhaseNum identity, a convention-aware directory rule and a single config read, plus two oracle-backed conformance fixtures.
