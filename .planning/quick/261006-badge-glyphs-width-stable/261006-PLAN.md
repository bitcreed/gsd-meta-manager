# Width-stable badge glyphs

Replace the paused and external-job badge glyphs U+23F8 and U+23F3, which render emoji-wide on many terminals and break column alignment, with width-stable `‖` (U+2016) and `◷` (U+25F7).

Record written retroactively; work shipped in a23b6ca.
