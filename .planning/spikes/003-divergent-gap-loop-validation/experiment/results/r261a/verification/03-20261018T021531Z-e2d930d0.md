### Gaps Summary

The 01-02 gap is closed: `'+'` is now spelled `plus`, its unit tests pass, and feed lines 22-27 pass. The phase still fails, though. The product team's feed grew again, from 27 to 33 lines, and lines 28-33 add a `&` -> `n` house-style rule that `slug.py` lacks. Because the feed keeps growing new rules, the SLUG-02 traceability note in REQUIREMENTS.md and the ROADMAP Phase 1 row should stay as they are (not Complete) until a run against the then-current feed exits 0. A closing plan should re-sync and re-check the feed at execution time. If the feed grows again, expect another round.
