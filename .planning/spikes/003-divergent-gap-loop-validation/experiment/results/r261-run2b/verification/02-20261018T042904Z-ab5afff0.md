## Gap Analysis

Plan 01-02 correctly closed the plus-sign convention (lines 22-27 at that time) with a general rule. The product team's sync has since grown the feed again to 33 lines. Lines 28-33 introduce a new category: titles containing an ampersand, where the expected slug writes the ampersand as the letter "n". With surrounding spaces the result is a hyphen-separated "n" word; with no spaces the "n" is glued to the neighbouring letters. All six lines are consistent with a single rule: replace the ampersand with "n" (no padding) before separator collapse. Feed contents are described by category only, per the plan's feed-handling rules.

This is not deferred to Phase 2 (CLI only); the roadmap contract is "for every line N of the synced feed". The feed is the authority (D-05).

Fix direction: add one general ampersand-to-"n" replacement in `slugkit/slug.py` ahead of separator collapse, update the existing punctuation/output-alphabet unit tests that currently use an ampersand as a dropped character, add a unit test with original strings, re-inspect all live feed lines, and do not touch `feed/` or `.gitignore`. Note the feed has grown twice since execution; a further regeneration may add more conventions, so re-inspect again at closure time.
