**The pattern call, made deliberately and recorded for each gap independently.**

Rounds 9, 10 and 11 each produced a fresh crop of over-claiming censuses, and each new
mechanism has needed certifying by the next round. So the default answer this round is *narrow
the claim and disclose the remainder*, and a mechanism change is admissible only where it is
**bounded and terminal** — where it cannot itself need certifying next round. Both items in
this plan clear that bar, and each clears it for its own reason:

- **gaps[1]: bounded mechanism FIX + narrowed claim.** The misplaced increment is not an
  over-claim; it is the mechanism failing to honour its own documented constant. The repair is
  terminal because the resulting window gets a **two-sided committed boundary test** — asserted
  at N and at N+1, in both directions — and a window with a two-sided boundary test IS its own
  certificate. Disclosure alone was rejected: this census is the only committed thing standing

  comment lines would leave the phase's sharpest execution-sink control knowingly blind. The
  claim is narrowed anyway, in the same commit: the word **any** comes out of the doc and the
  three real bounds go in.
- **gaps[2]: bounded mechanism FIX that DELETES the window from the decision + narrowed claim.**
  Over-joining is a **laundering** defect, not merely a narrow reach: the census silently misses
  the exact construction it exists to catch. The repair is terminal because the verdict moves

  **there is no window left to over-join**, so no future round can find a bigger unit that
  launders something. Its residual flips to over-detection, which is **loud**, and loud is the
  safe direction.

Neither is a new census. Neither adds a needle, a file set or a completeness claim. Prohibition

to narrow the claim instead, and the executor must stop and say so.
