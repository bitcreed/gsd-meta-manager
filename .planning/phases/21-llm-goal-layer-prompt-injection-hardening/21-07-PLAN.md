<assumption_delta_decision>
The deterministic detector fired (`kind: pluralization`) and the delta is real:
**`--goal` became a third command source where there were two**, and the way it
was added is exactly CR-01.

- **Primary noun:** *command source*. It has three variants — a supplied
  `--command`, a `--target-phase` under the router, and a stated `--goal`.
- **Decision: `promote`.** The three-way source becomes a named resolved value
  (`CommandSource`) that `drive` computes once and `preview_text` matches
  exhaustively. The old shape — three `Option`s re-matched at each consumer with
  a fall-through arm carrying a prose claim of unreachability — is what let the
  third variant be added alongside the other two without the preview learning
  about it. Adding alongside is what shipped the defect; promoting is the fix.
- **Invariant guard (accepted, planned as Task 2):** an enumerated test that
  every legal command source renders a scope-appropriate preview, so a future
  fourth source cannot fall through to the command-mode renderer while a test
  still passes.
</assumption_delta_decision>
