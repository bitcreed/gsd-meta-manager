<objective>
Close pass-6's gap 1 — the naming/identity property — by giving the tree the two things the
verifier proved it lacks: a predicate clause for "carries something different from what it
renders," and a fixture shape that can express it.

Five rounds fixed exactly the shapes the previous verifier had *reproduced* and each left a
shape that had only been *named*. Round 5 is the cleanest instance: pass 5's own report wrote
"two visually identical aliases resolve to two different envelope and credential paths," 21-15
truth 5 claimed to close it, and the fix could not — `carries_visible_content` asks "is anything
visible?", which refuses `"\u{200b}"` and accepts `"demo\u{200b}"` by construction. The 42-cell
matrix went green over the harm because all six `DEGENERATE` payloads are wholly invisible: no
fixture in the tree can express "renders identically, differs in bytes."

Per the pass-6 verifier's recommendation, treated as the design brief:

1. **The missing CLAUSE, not another consumer.** `text::carries_invisible_formatting` beside
   `carries_visible_content`, one shared char-class spelling. Consumed by
   `is_plain_path_component` (after its `is_control()` refusal), which closes the run-id,
   alias-at-seam, envelope, credential, and phase-token halves in one change.
2. **The missing FIXTURE SHAPE.** `LOOK_ALIKE_PAIRS` beside `DEGENERATE`: pairs that differ in
   bytes and agree on screen, asserted at every seam that turns a value into an identity.
3. **`Commands` becomes a classified domain the way `DriveArgs` did.** A validated
   `registry::Alias` newtype at the argv entry points (Add, the five envelope re-entries), a
   registration refusal that names the harm, an honest raw-by-design record for Remove, and
   guard ten's census so a ninth subcommand cannot add an alias field unclassified.
4. **The WR-06 falsehood corrected**: `AliasNotVisible` instead of a borrowed `UnknownAlias`.

Criterion 1 is verified for the first time in six passes: `from_argv` stays pure, stays above
`drive`, keeps its no-`..` destructure and its `NonBlank` fields. Nothing in this plan moves
that boundary; the only change at it is the alias refusal's VARIANT (Task 2a), which preserves
the verified shape — same site, same purity, same ordering — while correcting what the refusal
claims about the registry.
</objective>
