Both gaps live in `src/executor/claude.rs`, so no two plans touching it can share a wave. The split
is by **code region**, not by gap id: this plan owns the non-loop regions (`interrupt`, the
envelope collection, the post-loop derivation call, the run-end drain), and 15-08 owns the
supervisor `select!` loop proper. That keeps each plan's read-and-edit surface inside one coherent
region and inside the ~50% context budget, and it lands the smaller, self-contained fix first so
its regression test is green before the riskier loop surgery starts. Traceability to the two gaps
is preserved by the `must_haves.truths` annotations above, each of which names the
`15-VERIFICATION.md` `missing:` bullet it closes.
