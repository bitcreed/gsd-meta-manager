<objective>
`gsd-meta-manager driver new <project> <slug>` mints a run dir from embedded templates - self-ignoring
`.gitignore`, DIRECTION.md with the human body and the Phase 27 emitter's never-launched frontmatter, LOG.md
with its grammar inline - and `driver check` lints the DIRECTION body into a machine-checkable plan the human
reviews before any launch (PRMT-02, DRIVE-03, PRMT-03 template half; D-08 .. D-12, D-15, D-16).

Purpose: a new run is one command from a ready DIRECTION.md (ROADMAP goal); the lint is reused by the launch
preflight in plan 29-06.

Output: `src/driver_templates/` (module, lint, two assets), the `driver new` / `driver check` verbs, e2e tests.
</objective>
