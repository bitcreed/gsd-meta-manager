<objective>
From the TUI inbox, the owner settles a whole phase row in a few keys: `x`, then `w` (won't do, with a reason) or `p` (deferred permanently), then a default-No confirm popup that shows the exact argv, and Yes launches `/gsd-verify-work N <instruction>` in the project through the existing launch path. GSD records the outcome; the app writes nothing.

Purpose: OWN-29 (actions on a phase row in the inbox; check rows and the Owner block follow in 39-02), OWN-30 (a GSD command with a prepared plain-language instruction behind a confirm popup) and the first proof for OWN-31 (the tracer flow runs on a throwaway fixture copy whose tree must stay byte-identical). The instruction uses gsd-core's own deferral encoding (`result: skipped`, reason beginning `Deferred follow-up:`), the only skip gsd-core and our reader treat as non-blocking (RESEARCH Pitfall 1), and it is sanitized for verify-work's `sed | xargs` argument parse (Pitfalls 2 and 3).

Output: two new modules (`owner_settle.rs` pure core, `settle_popup.rs` screens), argv builders in `detail.rs`, the `x` arm in `inbox.rs`, the `command_phase` helper, escape-guard fixtures, tests.

Decisions implemented here: D-01 (Won't do, Deferred permanently; Move is 39-02), D-02, D-03, D-05 (key and chooser; footer and help are 39-03), D-07, D-08 (Won't do and Deferred templates, phase and check refs), D-10, D-11 (focus half), D-12, D-13 (tracer half).
