<objective>
Teach the ROADMAP reader the three things the new Roadmap tab needs and today's parser does not
know: a phase's **Goal** (D-A03), ttbook's `#### Build phase N (Milestone M): Title` placeholder
phases (D-A12, success criterion 4), and enough milestone facts to draw each milestone once as a
band with shipped milestones collapsed into one row (D-A07). Vendor sanitised structural excerpts
of the three real roadmaps that exhibit the defects (D-A14) so every later plan can pin them.

Purpose: the layout engine (24-02) and the Roadmap adapter (24-05) read only in-memory
`ProjectState`; this plan puts the missing facts there without changing anything GSD-facing.

**How build phases "parse as phases" and stay selectable (orchestrator requirement 5):** the new
parser returns each build-phase heading as an ordinary `RoadmapPhase` value (number, name, plan
counts, dependencies) in `ProjectState.planned_phases`. Plan 24-05's adapter lists
`state.phases` followed by `state.planned_phases` as list nodes, so the Roadmap cursor (j/k, g/G,
h/l, [/]) stops on them and the detail pane shows their Goal/Needs/Unblocks/Parallel exactly like
any other phase; only `Enter` differs (a status message, because the Phases tab lists GSD phases).
They are kept out of `state.phases` because GSD's own heading grammar does not match `Build phase`
and ttbook's roadmap says they "are not GSD phases" — merging them would make the driver router
and frontier target a phase GSD does not know (RESEARCH Pattern 3b). [INFERRED — audit]

Dependency: none — wave 1. Files are disjoint from 24-02 (`src/ui/roadmap_graph.rs`) and 24-03
(`src/app.rs`, `src/ui/screens/detail.rs`, `src/ui/screens/render_escape_guard.rs`).

Output: three new `roadmap_md` functions (plus a count helper), three new `ProjectState` fields,
seven fixture files.
</objective>
