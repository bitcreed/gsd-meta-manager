<objective>
Steer a launcher run from the Driver tab by editing its DIRECTION.md body: set `control: run|pause|stop`, answer
or un-hold the item under the cursor, and edit the body in `$VISUAL`/`$EDITOR` through a body-only temp with a
no-clobber apply. Every write goes through `write_body` and the shared `DIRECTION.lock`; the Phase 18 injection key
is retired (D-02, D-05, D-14, D-15, D-16, D-17, D-22 polite stop, D-24 edit temp, D-25, D-27, D-28).

Purpose: SC3, and SC4's "the Phase 18 stream-json injection UI is no longer the steering path".

Output: the pure transforms and the two blocking helpers in `src/driver/steer.rs`, the steering screens in
`src/ui/screens/driver_steer.rs`, `App::after_editor`, the main-loop exit-status hand-off, and their tests.
</objective>
