<objective>
Start, launch and relaunch launcher runs from the Driver tab: `R` relaunches a crashed (or stopped, parked,
stalled, paused) run or launches a new one through a confirmation that shows the caps and the spend ceiling, and
`s` mints a new run, opens its DIRECTION.md in the editor, checks it, then offers the launch. Launching goes
through the launcher library's detached path; degraded hosts need a separate unsandboxed confirmation (D-02 start
half, D-18, D-19, D-20, D-21, D-26).

Purpose: SC4's "start a new run (mint id, edit DIRECTION, launch)" and "relaunch a crashed one".

Output: `src/ui/screens/driver_launch.rs`, the clap-backed defaults in `src/cli.rs`, the `LaunchSeams` and launch
actions in `src/app.rs`, `tests/driver_tab_launch_e2e.rs`, and their tests.
</objective>
