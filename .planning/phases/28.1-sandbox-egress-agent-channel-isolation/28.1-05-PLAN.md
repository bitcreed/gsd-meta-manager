<objective>
Give the sandbox its one way out: the hidden in-sandbox `bridge` subcommand (D-08) that binds
`127.0.0.1:<port>`, forwards every accepted connection to the bound-in egress socket of 28.1-01, runs
the driver as its child and hands the child's exit code back to bwrap.

Purpose: 28.1-10 runs this bridge as bwrap's command inside the network-isolated profile and proves
SC1 live; 28.1-07 wires both into `driver launch`. This plan proves the bridge itself in CI, against a
real in-process `EgressServer`. (Split from the original 28.1-05 on checker scope_sanity feedback: the
profile, env contract and live proofs moved to 28.1-10 as one capability slice, not by layer
`[inferred — audit]`.)

Output: `bridge.rs`, a `process.rs` spawn helper, the hidden CLI subcommand with its pre-tracing
dispatch, and the CI test `tests/launcher_bridge.rs`.

| bridge -> egress socket | the bridge only relays bytes; policy and the token live in the launcher-side EgressServer (28.1-01) |
