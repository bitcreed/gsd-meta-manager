<objective>
Close the two reader defects before a single routing rule is widened.

Research measured both and named them the phase's biggest correctness risk. This repository's
status vocabulary has no `Executed` state while GSD's does, and its `Complete` means only
*implementation complete* — GSD's `executed` — because its first derivation arm is exactly GSD's
`implementation_complete` predicate. Separately, the inference records verification-artifact
*presence* as a boolean and never reads the frontmatter status, and that status is the entire
DRIVE-05 gate set. A router built on the reader as it stands walks straight past a phase whose
verification is `human_needed`: the precise gate it exists to park at.

This plan extends the one reader — never a parallel one (D-01, D-11) — with GSD's vocabulary and
with every human-judgement gate that is observable from disk. It also carries the trap
`20-PATTERNS.md` flagged: the status enum derives its ordering from declaration order, so the new
variant is *inserted*, never appended.

Purpose: the router's disposition can only be as correct as what the reader can see. Everything in
plan 20-04 rests on this.
Output: GSD's eight-state vocabulary, a typed verification status, the disk-observable gate set,
declared roadmap dependencies, and every match site updated by compile error rather than by search.
</objective>
