## Accomplishments

- **Seed v2 trigger.** `verifier_spawns(lines)` returns `[(timestamp, tool_use_id)]` for `Agent`/`Task` tool_use blocks whose `input.subagent_type` is `gsd-verifier`. `SpawnScanner` reads `<cfg>/projects/*/*.jsonl` and `<cfg>/projects/*/*/subagents/**/agent-*.jsonl` incrementally (byte offset per file, complete lines only, ids de-duplicated). In verifier mode `start()` logs `start gen=<k> trigger=verifier`, and `poll()` logs `gen=<k> summaries=<n> head=<sha> titles=<t> trigger=verifier spawn=<id8> spawn_at=<ts>`; past MAX_GEN it logs `exhausted spawns=<n>`. SUMMARY commits never move the feed in this mode. Summary mode is untouched, so its log lines are byte-identical. GEN0/GENS/_CLASSES are byte-unchanged against c8a8a33.
- **CLI.** `watch --trigger {summary,verifier} --claude-config DIR` (verifier without a config exits 2 via argparse); `extract-spawns CLAUDE_CONFIG` prints one `{"type","timestamp","message"}` skeleton line per Agent/Task tool_use of any subagent type.
- **Privacy.** Only `id`, `name`, `input.subagent_type` and the line `timestamp` are taken, each checked against a strict pattern (so a hostile value cannot inject a newline into the log). The selftest plants `PLANTED-SECRET-c0ffee` in message text and in a spawn's prompt and asserts it appears in no log line, snapshot, or `extract-spawns` output.
- **Fixture.** `experiment/replay/run2-spawn-skeleton.jsonl` came from the scratch root (`extract-spawns` over r261-run2's claude-config; the precondition held, no hand-written fallback). It holds exactly the five planned tuples; one gsd-verifier line at 2026-10-18T02:33:31.810Z after the gsd-executor line at 2026-10-18T02:31:05.029Z. No secret-pattern hits.
- **Selftest (drift).** Synthetic tree: executor spawn no advance; verifier line without newline no advance; newline gives gen 1 and ` trigger=verifier spawn=`; a following `status: gaps_found` VERIFICATION is snapshotted `gen=1`; a duplicate id (second transcript) is ignored; a SUMMARY commit does not advance; a `Task`-named second spawn gives gen 2; `watch --once --trigger verifier` restart logs `start gen=2 trigger=verifier`, no new advance and no second snapshot; ten spawns reach `exhausted spawns=10`; a missing cfg dir is gen 0 without error; every advance line in both modes matches `^(\S+) gen=(\d+) summaries=(\d+)`. Prints `selftest OK gens=9`.
- **WR-03.** `gapcount.STATUS_RE` and `fm_status(raw)` (bytes or str, frontmatter-only, quote-tolerant). drift.py dropped its private `_STATUS_RE` and imports gapcount (tools dir added to `sys.path`, as `python3 -I` drops it).
